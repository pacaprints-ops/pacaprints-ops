# Social Media Best Practices
_Last researched: July 2026. Review every 6 months as platforms update._

---

## Instagram

### Image & Video Specs

| Format         | Dimensions     | Aspect Ratio | Notes                                      |
|----------------|----------------|--------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Feed photo     | 1080 x 1080px  | 1:1          | Square — most reliable in grid             |
| Feed portrait  | 1080 x 1350px  | 4:5          | Taller = more screen space, higher engage  |
| Stories        | 1080 x 1920px  | 9:16         | Full screen vertical                       |
| Reels          | 1080 x 1920px  | 9:16         | Required — square/horizontal is penalised  |
| Reel safe zone | 1010 x 1440px  | —            | Keep all text/faces inside this zone       |

**Reel safe zone detail:** Avoid top 220px (username/profile area) and bottom 450px (captions/buttons). Keep critical content in the centre 1010×1440px region.

**Video format:** MP4 or MOV, H.264 codec, 30fps, audio AAC.

### Reels Length
- **7–30 seconds** — hook-driven clips, best for discovery
- **30–90 seconds** — content with depth, storytelling, product features

### Captions
- First 125 characters show before "more" — put the hook there
- Front-load keywords (Instagram is increasingly search-driven)
- End with CTA before hashtags
- 3–5 hashtags max — niche, relevant, placed at the end

### Hashtags (Instagram)
- 3–5 well-chosen niche hashtags outperform 30 broad ones
- Use a mix: brand hashtag + product niche + occasion/seasonal
- Instagram search uses keywords in captions, not just hashtags — write naturally

### Strategy (Instagram)
- **80/20 rule:** 80% value/lifestyle content, 20% promotional
- Post 3–5 feed posts per week + daily Stories for best reach
- Engage with comments quickly — early engagement boosts algorithmic reach
- Use Collab posts to share reach with complementary accounts
- Instagram Shopping: tag products in every product post
- Add alt text to every image for accessibility and SEO
- Micro-influencer partnerships (5k–50k) outperform celebrity endorsements for small businesses

## TikTok

### Video Specs

| Setting        | Recommended                        |
|----------------|------------------------------------|
| Resolution     | 1080 x 1920px                      |
| Aspect ratio   | 9:16 (vertical — fills full screen)|
| Format         | MP4, H.264, AAC audio              |
| Frame rate     | 30fps (talking head), 60fps (fast motion) |
| Bitrate        | 8–16 Mbps                          |
| Max file size  | 287.6MB                            |
| Max length     | Up to 10 minutes (most accounts)   |

### Video Length (Sweet Spots)
- **11–18 seconds** — viral trends, quick tips, punchy entertainment
- **21–34 seconds** — product demos, storytelling, testimonials
- **30–60 seconds** — how-tos, educational, deeper value

### Captions (TikTok)
- Keep captions under 150 characters — hook first, then hashtags
- Include 3–5 niche hashtags that match your content (not viral tags for the sake of it)
- End with a CTA: comment, follow, check link in bio
- **On-screen text is critical:** 80%+ of TikTok is watched silently — always add captions/text overlay to video

### Hashtags (TikTok)
- 3–5 niche/relevant hashtags per post
- Avoid chasing viral/irrelevant hashtags — it confuses the algorithm and attracts the wrong audience
- Mix: brand hashtag + niche product tag + occasion/event tag
- Good starting point for Paca: `#pacaprints` `#uksmallbusiness` `#ukgiftshop` `#tiktokmademebuyit` `#smallbusinessuk`

### Hook Strategy
- First 1–3 seconds determine whether someone stays or scrolls — this is the most important part of any TikTok
- Ask a question, make a bold statement, or show something visually arresting immediately
- Text overlay hook + visual hook together = best results

### TikTok Shop
- Link products to TikTok Shop in every product video
- Use LIVE Shopping for seasonal pushes and new product launches
- Product showcase tab should always be up to date

### Strategy (TikTok)
- Post 1–3x per day ideally; consistency beats volume
- Use trending sounds (where appropriate) to boost discoverability
- Optimise for social search: include keywords naturally in captions AND spoken audio
- Prioritise human-centric, authentic content over polished ads
- Behind-the-scenes content performs well for small makers

---

## Universal Rules (Both Platforms)

- **Consistency beats perfection** — regular posting matters more than the perfect post
- **Reply to comments** — engagement signals boost reach on both platforms
- **Keywords everywhere** — captions, alt text (Instagram), on-screen text (TikTok) are all searchable
- **Repurpose smartly** — one piece of content → Instagram feed + Stories + TikTok with minor tweaks
- **Test and learn** — use native analytics to see what your specific audience responds to
