# Canva Brief — 18th Birthday Cards (Instagram Feed Post)

**Canvas size:** 1080 × 1080px

---

## Why this design works

Most birthday card content on Instagram is soft, pastel, and forgettable. This design goes the opposite direction — dark, bold, and typographic. The card titles ARE the product. A parent scrolling Instagram who has a kid turning 18 will stop immediately when they read "Your Childhood Has Expired."

---

## Background

- Colour: Deep charcoal `#1e1e1e`
- No gradients — solid and confident

---

## Background accent (large faded "18")

- Text: `18`
- Font: Any heavy display font (e.g. Montserrat Black, Impact, Bebas Neue)
- Size: ~700–800px (enormous — fills most of the canvas)
- Colour: White at **8% opacity** — very faint, just visible, acts as a watermark effect
- Position: Centred on canvas, vertically centred
- This gives depth without cluttering

---

## Layout (top to bottom)

### 1. Brand label — top centre
- Text: `PACA PRINTS`
- Font: Montserrat Light or similar clean sans-serif
- Size: 28px
- Colour: Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Letter spacing: 5–6px
- Position: Centred, 60px from top

### 2. Mint divider line — below brand label
- A thin horizontal line, 120px wide, 2px tall
- Colour: Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Position: Centred, ~110px from top

### 3. Stack of card titles — centre of canvas (this is the hero)

Three lines stacked, centre-aligned, with generous line spacing:

**Line 1:**
- Text: `Your Childhood`
- Font: Playfair Display Bold Italic or similar expressive serif
- Size: ~90px
- Colour: White `#ffffff`

**Line 2:**
- Text: `Has Expired.`
- Same font, same size
- Colour: Peach `#f7d9c4`

*(small gap — ~24px)*

**Line 3:**
- Text: `You're On Your Own.`
- Same font, size ~70px (slightly smaller so it fits)
- Colour: White `#ffffff`

*(small gap — ~24px)*

**Line 4:**
- Text: `You're an Actual Adult Now.`
- Same font, size ~58px
- Colour: White `#ffffff` at 75% opacity (slightly faded — creates a staggered depth effect)

All four lines should sit roughly between 280px and 720px from the top of the canvas.

### 4. Price tag / badge — below card titles
- Shape: Rounded rectangle pill, ~340px wide × 54px tall
- Background: Peach `#f7d9c4`
- Text: `Cards from £3.49 · personalised too`
- Font: Montserrat Regular or Medium
- Size: ~24px
- Text colour: Charcoal `#1e1e1e`
- Position: Centred, ~760px from top

### 5. CTA line — near bottom
- Text: `Shop the collection — link in bio`
- Font: Montserrat Regular
- Size: ~26px
- Colour: Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Position: Centred, ~880px from top

### 6. Bottom mint divider line
- Same as the top divider: 120px wide, 2px, Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Position: Centred, ~940px from top

---

## Product photo (optional)

This design works without a product photo — the typography IS the creative. However, if you want to add one:
- Place a small photo of 2–3 cards fanned out in the bottom-left or bottom-right corner
- Crop to a circle or soft rounded rectangle
- Keep it small — ~260×260px — don't let it compete with the text
- Add a very subtle mint border around it

---

## What to avoid
- Do not use light or pastel backgrounds — the dark is intentional and is what makes this stand out
- Do not use thin or light-weight fonts — everything should feel bold and confident
- Do not centre-align AND left-align elements on the same canvas — keep everything centred
- Do not add clip art or stock illustrations — the type alone is the design
