# Canva Brief — 21st Birthday Cards (Instagram Feed Post)

**Canvas size:** 1080 × 1080px

---

## Why this works

The 21st is THE birthday. These cards have a lovely feminine, celebratory aesthetic (bows, pink, bubbles) that fits perfectly with the brand's warm peach and mint palette. The concept is "keeper not binnable" — these look special enough to keep, not just send.

---

## Background

- Full canvas: Peach `#f7d9c4`
- Top section accent: Mint `#b8e0d2` — soft curved shape (like a gentle hill/arch) covering approximately the top 28% of the canvas, with a smooth curved bottom edge

---

## Logo — top centre

- File: `PacaPrints Logo .png`
- Width: ~240px
- Position: Horizontally centred, ~30px from top
- The logo has a white background — use "Remove background" in Canva or set blend mode to Multiply to sit cleanly on the mint panel

---

## Text elements

### 1. Sacramento script label (beneath logo)
- Text: `21st Birthday Collection`
- Font: **Sacramento**, regular
- Size: ~50px
- Colour: `#3d2c00` (dark warm brown)
- Position: Centred, immediately below logo, ~230px from top

### 2. Varela Round headline
- Text: `The card they'll actually want to keep. 🎀`
- Font: **Varela Round**, regular (the font is naturally bold-looking)
- Size: ~68px
- Colour: `#3d2c00`
- Alignment: Centre
- Position: Centred, ~300px–430px from top
- Allow text to wrap to two lines naturally

Place three card images centred across the canvas, starting at approximately 455px from top.

## CTA pill — bottom

- Shape: Rounded pill / rectangle (border-radius: 60px)
- Background: Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Text: `Cards from £3.49 · Shop now ↓`
- Font: **Varela Round**, regular
- Size: ~30px
- Text colour: `#3d2c00`
- Width: ~520px
- Position: Horizontally centred, ~56px from bottom
- Soft shadow: `0 4px 12px` warm brown at 12% opacity

---

## What to avoid

- Do not use pure black (`#000000`) anywhere — use dark warm brown `#3d2c00`
- Do not add sharp corners to anything
- Do not crowd the cards — give them space to breathe
- Do not use more than two fonts
- Do not add too many decorative elements — the cards are the star
