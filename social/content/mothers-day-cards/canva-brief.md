# Canva Brief — Mother's Day Cards (Instagram Feed Post)

**Canvas size:** 1080 × 1080px (square feed post)

---

## Background

- Solid colour: Peach `#f7d9c4`
- No gradient needed — keep it soft and clean

---

## Layout (top to bottom)

### 1. Brand label — top centre
- Text: `PACA PRINTS`
- Font: Clean sans-serif, light weight (e.g. Montserrat Light or similar)
- Size: ~28–32px
- Colour: Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Position: Centred, approx 60px from top edge

### 2. Product image — centre
- A photo of your Mother's Day card(s) — ideally a flat lay
- Suggested styling: cards fanned or stacked slightly, placed centrally on the canvas
- Leave breathing room around the image (don't let it touch the edges)
- If you have multiple cards, fan them out slightly so all are visible
- Image should occupy roughly 55–60% of the canvas height

### 3. Headline — below image
- Text: `Cards that actually say something.`
- Font: Serif or friendly bold sans-serif (e.g. Playfair Display Bold or Poppins Bold)
- Size: ~52–60px
- Colour: Dark charcoal `#3a3a3a`
- Alignment: Centre
- Position: Approx 60px below the product image

### 4. Sub-text — below headline
- Text: `Mother's Day Cards — Shop at pacaprints.com`
- Font: Same sans-serif as brand label, regular weight
- Size: ~26px
- Colour: `#3a3a3a` at ~70% opacity (or medium grey)
- Alignment: Centre

### 5. Thin border (optional but recommended)
- A thin rectangular border inset ~30px from all four canvas edges
- Colour: Mint `#b8e0d2`
- Stroke width: 2–3px
- Gives the post a polished, framed feel

---

## Decorative accents (optional)
- A small sprig of leaves or simple floral illustration in one corner (mint/peach tones)
- Alternatively, a small heart icon near the headline in mint

---

## Things to avoid
- Don't let text overlap the product photo
- Don't use more than two fonts
- Don't add too many elements — clean and uncluttered is the goal
