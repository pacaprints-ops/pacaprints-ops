## Voice & Tone

- **Humorous but professional** — warm, friendly, never cold or corporate
- **British English always** — no American spellings (colour not color, personalised not personalized)
- **Never robotic or generic** — write like a real person, not a marketing bot
- **Never pushy or salesy** — lead with value and warmth, let the product speak

---

## Brand Fonts

Both are Google Fonts — free to use in Canva, on web, and in any design tool.

| Font | Use | Style |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Varela Round** | Main headers, headlines, bold statements | Rounded, friendly, modern sans-serif |
| **Sacramento** | Sub-headers, accents, decorative text | Elegant script/handwriting |

- Headlines and post titles → Varela Round (bold, all caps or title case)
- Secondary text, captions on-image, decorative labels → Sacramento
- Body copy and small text → any clean sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial, or Varela Round Regular)

**In Canva:** Both are available under "Brand fonts" → search "Varela Round" and "Sacramento"
**In HTML/CSS:** Load from Google Fonts: `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Varela+Round&family=Sacramento&display=swap`

## Content Pillars

Use a mix of these across the week. Aim for 80% value/lifestyle, 20% promotional.

1. **Products** — styled shots of prints, cards, and gifts
2. **Behind the Scenes** — making, printing, packing, Cricut work
3. **Seasonal / Occasions** — Mother's Day, Christmas, Teacher gifts, etc.
4. **Promotions** — sales, discount codes, TikTok Shop deals
5. **Lifestyle / Relatable** — gifting ideas, home décor, humorous takes

## Caption Rules

### Both Platforms
- First line must be a scroll-stopper hook — make it punchy
- Warm and slightly humorous tone throughout
- Always include a clear CTA (call to action)
- British English only
- Never use more than 5 hashtags

### Instagram Captions
- First 125 characters visible before "more" — front-load the hook
- 3–5 niche hashtags, placed at the end of the caption
- Tag products using Instagram Shopping where relevant
- Add alt text to every post for accessibility and search

### TikTok Captions
- Keep under 150 characters where possible (hook + hashtags)
- 3–5 relevant, niche hashtags — avoid chasing irrelevant viral tags
- End with a CTA: follow, comment, check link in bio, etc.
- Add on-screen text/captions to every video (80%+ of views are silent)

## Hashtag Guidance

Use niche and relevant tags. Avoid mass-appeal ones that attract the wrong audience.

**Good examples for Paca Prints:**
`#pacaprints` `#uksmallbusiness` `#printshop` `#greetingcards` `#giftideasuk`
`#personalizedgifts` `#wallart` `#ukgifts` `#mothersdaygift` `#tiktokmademebuyit`

---

## Always Do

- Reply to comments within 24 hours
- Tag products in Instagram Shopping posts
- Use alt text on every Instagram post
- Repurpose content — Instagram feed → Stories → TikTok where it fits
- Match the caption energy to the visual (funny visual = funny caption)

## Never Do

- Post without a clear purpose or CTA
- Use American spellings
- Copy generic captions from templates without adapting the tone
- Use hashtags unrelated to the content just to chase views
- Post low-resolution images
