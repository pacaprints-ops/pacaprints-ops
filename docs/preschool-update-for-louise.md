## What's New: September 2027 Enrolments Page

We've added a new **Enrolments** section to the app (in the admin menu). This replaces the old waiting list and is specifically for planning the September 2027 intake.

### How it works

**Returning children** are pulled in automatically — the app looks at every child currently registered and works out which ones won't be starting primary school in September 2027. They appear at the top of the list without you having to do anything.

**New starters** can be added manually using the "+ Add Child" button. When you add a child you record:
- Child's name and date of birth
- Parent / carer name, phone number and email
- Which days they need and what session (morning, afternoon, or full day)

The page shows the same Mon–Fri hours layout as the Termly Register, so you can see at a glance what the September picture looks like across the week.

### Fees

When you click on any new starter's name, their details expand and you'll see two fee buttons you can mark as paid:

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Welcome fee | £50 | Non-refundable |
| Deposit | £50 | Returned on their first invoice automatically |

Just tap the button to mark it paid — it turns green. The deposit will be deducted from the child's first term invoice automatically once they're fully enrolled.
